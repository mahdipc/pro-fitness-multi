# Progression Rules

## Double Progression Default

For most resistance exercises:

- prescribe a rep range, e.g. 8-12 reps;
- if the user completes all sets at the top of the rep range with RPE <= 8, increase load next time;
- increase load by a small amount, usually 2.5-5% for upper body and 5-10% for lower body when appropriate;
- if load cannot be increased, add 1-2 reps or slow tempo.

## RPE Guidance

- Beginner: most sets RPE 6-8.
- Intermediate: most sets RPE 7-9.
- Avoid regular RPE 10 unless advanced and specifically justified.

## Pain Rule

- Sharp pain: stop the movement.
- Joint pain that worsens: replace exercise or reduce range/load.
- Normal muscle soreness is not automatically a reason to stop, but severe soreness may require lower volume.

## Fatigue Adjustment

Reduce next session volume by 20-40% if:

- sleep is very poor for several nights;
- performance drops strongly for two sessions;
- resting fatigue is high;
- motivation and soreness indicate poor recovery.

## Cardio Progression

Increase cardio duration gradually:

- add 5 minutes per session or 10-15 minutes per week;
- avoid increasing intensity and duration at the same time;
- if recovery suffers, reduce cardio intensity first.
