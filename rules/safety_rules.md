# Safety Rules

These are deterministic safety rules. They must override agent preferences.

## Medical Disclaimer Behavior

The system must not diagnose, treat, or replace medical advice. It can provide general fitness guidance.

## Red Flags Requiring Medical Review Before Intense Exercise

If any of these are present, the system must avoid intense exercise and recommend medical evaluation before starting or continuing hard training:

- chest pain, pressure, or unexplained tightness during activity
- fainting, dizziness, or loss of consciousness
- severe shortness of breath at rest or with light activity
- oxygen saturation reported as low or unstable
- uncontrolled high blood pressure
- uncontrolled diabetes symptoms or severe hypoglycemia episodes
- new neurological symptoms
- acute severe joint pain
- acute back pain with neurological symptoms
- recent surgery without clearance
- pregnancy/postpartum status with unclear clearance
- fever, acute infection, or serious illness

## Injury Rules

- Knee pain: avoid mandatory deep squats, jumps, and high-impact intervals unless cleared.
- Lower-back pain: avoid mandatory heavy deadlifts, heavy good mornings, and loaded spinal flexion.
- Shoulder pain: avoid mandatory overhead press, dips, or painful bench variations.
- Wrist/elbow pain: avoid mandatory painful push-up or barbell variations.

## Beginner Safety Defaults

- Start with RPE 6-8.
- Avoid 1RM testing.
- Avoid forced reps.
- Avoid high-intensity interval training if cardiovascular status is unclear.
- Prefer machine, dumbbell, cable, bodyweight, or controlled variations when technique is unknown.

## Final Output Safety Note

Every `WorkoutPlan.fa.md` must include:

- stop exercise if sharp pain, chest pain, dizziness, or abnormal shortness of breath occurs;
- consult a physician/physiotherapist for serious medical issues or injuries;
- this plan is general fitness guidance, not medical treatment.
